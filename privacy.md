# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

## 1. Who this applies to

Kite Clipper is a private, single-user tool run by one person (the operator, GitHub: safetykite). It has no sign-up and no other users. This policy explains what data the tool handles.

## 2. What data the tool handles

The tool handles only the operator's own account data: access tokens that the operator's TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts issue to the tool, and the videos, captions and public post statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of the operator's own posts. It does not collect personal data from any other person.

## 3. Where it is stored

Everything is stored on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with any third party. There is no advertising and no analytics.

## 4. Who data goes to

Videos and captions are sent only to the platform the operator chose to post on (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube), through that platform's official API, so that they can be published on the operator's own account.

## 5. Deleting data and revoking access

The operator can revoke the tool's access at any time in the security or connected-apps settings of each platform, and can delete the stored tokens and files from the operator's computer.
