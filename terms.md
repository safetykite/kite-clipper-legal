Kite Clipper is a private, single-user tool. It prepares short videos and uploads them to social media accounts that are owned and operated by one person (the operator, GitHub: safetykite). It is not a public service. It has no sign-up and no other users, and it is not offered to the public.

## 3. How it posts

The tool uses the official APIs of the platforms it posts to (TikTok, Instagram and YouTube). It only posts to the operator's own accounts, after the operator has approved the content. It does not like, follow, comment on or message other people's accounts.
